How it Began

A digital agency for brands with big ambitions

Partnering with i3MEDIA, proudly independent and fiercely results-driven, means working with a team that gets it done—no fluff, no hassle.

With over 20 years of experience, we bring bold ideas and sharp execution to every project, whether it’s crafting a show-stopping website, driving leads with SEO, or igniting big-impact campaigns across social media and PPC.

Our agency isn’t just a workplace—it’s where passion, creativity, and shared values come together. We know that remarkable work happens when collaboration thrives, respect is mutual, and excellence is the standard. By working closely with our clients, we turn their visions into bold realities, pushing limits and creating standout results. If you’re after an agency that’s as driven and dedicated as you are, look no further—you’ve found us.

Our Story

Shaping brands & digital experiences since ‘04

2004

Humble Beginnings

In 2004, i3MEDIA was founded in the humble surroundings of Azeem’s mother’s front room, armed with a clear vision and an Apple Powerbook G4, Azeem’s mission was simple but ambitious: to build more than just another digital agency. He aimed to transform businesses by delivering innovative, results-driven bespoke digital solutions.
