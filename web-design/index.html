Web Design Steps

Our website
design process

Project discovery & creative R&D

Our team will do a creative workshop with you to understand your needs. From this, we’ll crawl and audit current sitemaps before optimising pages and look at core page blocks to create a skeleton structure. We’ll also find inspiration for the visuals to get your site looking perfect.

Collaborative design phase

We collaborate with you during this phase to keep you in the loop and make a site tailored to your needs. Our team ask for your feedback to bring your vision to life for a site you love. We use Figma to create a bespoke design you can’t get anywhere else.

Development & early QA

Once you’re happy with our templates, our team will build your site on our secure WordPress staging platform, making the vision a reality. After this, we’ll carry out an initial website review and compare it to your agreed designs. This tests the site’s functionality and visual consistency while checking for bugs.

Population & final QA

We’ll start populating the content into each template to create working page structures for every type of page and media source. After this, it’s handed over to our QA team, who test all website components, responsiveness and usability. If there are any issues, our development team will fix them instantly.

Client hand back & CMS training

Our team will hand the project back for you to review and approve. We’ll also help you understand your site by showing the core processes, page management and best practices of the CMS. For charities using our Pillar platform, we’ll also include training on the CRM and donor management system.

Go live & early life support

After this, our team will configure the domain and VM for the live environment and the site will go live. We’ll do a post QA to retest the website’s functionality and visuals. Our team will offer 30 days of support after going live to fix errors and bugs or answer any questions.

Website Design FAQ's

Your questions, answered.

What platforms do you use for website design?
As a full-service web design and development agency, we don’t just offer one platform to build the perfect site for you. Our team offers far more than WordPress to provide a solution that meets your needs and works for you and your customers. We can build your site on WordPress, Pillar (for charities and the 3rd sector), WooCommerce, Shopify and many other open-source solutions.
What’s included in your creative services?
We offer creative services like no other to give you the best solution possible for your needs. These include our bespoke branding service that instantly transform dated sites into modern, stylish designs that showcase your brand and build your reputation. Alongside this, our creative services include flawless design services and asset creation.
Do you offer support after the site’s live?
We offer a range of support retainer services and cloud hosting and security support to maintain the quality of every page. Our cloud hosting and security support allows your site to perform optimally during peak periods. You get fast, secure and scalable hosting and Cloudflare, a personal dedicated account manager, security updates, patches and plugins and website backups. Our other support services include a website retainer that covers updates, creative services and performance management, as well as a marketing retainer to cover SEO, social, email, content and PR.
Can I get marketing support with your web design and development services?
Absolutely! We’re much more than an experienced web design agency. Not only can we build a functional, beautiful site, but we can also get it in front of the right people with our expert marketing solutions. When you combine our services, you gain page value reports to extend the sitemap planning, optimisations for your existing content and its structure and regular check-ins with your very own Account Manager who supports you every step of the way and keeps your site visible.
