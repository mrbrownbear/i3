Branding FAQs

Your questions, answered.

How much does a brand project cost?
Sadly, there are no set project costs. Every brand design we work on has different requirements that alter the spec of the job. Once we have received the brief, we will be able to provide you with a cost estimate and a timeline. The level of service may determine the fee that each branding agency charges.
Can you rebrand my existing business?
Absolutely. Whether it’s a complete overhaul or a subtle refresh, we can review your brand strategy and creative direction to help elevate your brand to the next level.
Do you work with startups or small businesses?
Of course! We love working with businesses of all sizes and appreciate the challenges for new or small enterprises. We’ve helped many new businesses find their voice.
How do I know if I need a rebrand?
Do you feel your current branding isn’t reflecting your business’ goals, image or audience? Then it’s time for something new. Let’s talk and we’ll see how we can help.
